About

My Path Into Data

I didn't start out in tech. I studied Biochemistry (BSc, 2:1) at King's College London, where I developed a strong foundation in analytical thinking through hands-on lab work — gel electrophoresis, PCR, chromatography — and computational biology, using tools like R, Unix, AlphaFold, and the Integrative Genomics Viewer to analyse genomic and protein data.
It was during my final two years at King's and my bioinformatics-focused summer internship that I discovered my interest in working with data. Applying coding and statistical methods to real laboratory datasets made me realise that what I enjoyed most was not just the science itself, but the process of taking complex, unstructured data and transforming it into something structured, reliable and meaningful. That experience ultimately led me to pursue a career in data.

What I do Now

Today, I work across data engineering, analytics and AI, with a focus on building the foundations that allow organisations to make better use of their data. I enjoy working with data from end to end, from understanding and transforming raw information to building reliable pipelines and creating structured datasets that can support analytics, reporting and AI applications. I am particularly interested in solving complex problems, improving the quality and accessibility of data, and finding ways to automate processes so that data can be used more efficiently and effectively.
  • Designing and maintaining data architecture — building the pipelines and systems that collect, store, and process data so it's ready for analysts and data scientists to use
  • Building reliable data pipelines — ensuring data flows correctly and accurately, end to end from data ingestion to analysis
  • Preparing data for modelling and analytics — transforming complex datasets into clean, structured and analysis-ready data for analytical and machine learning use cases.
  • Preparing data for modelling — getting datasets ready for analysis for predictive and prescriptive use cases
  • Automating workflows — identifying manual processes and developing automated solutions to improve efficiency, consistency and scalability.

Outside of work, I love playing volleyball and spending time with friends.
